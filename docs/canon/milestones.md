# Milestone candidates — DRAFT for Rob to cut

**Status:** proposed, 2026-08-14. Nothing here is decided. The job is to cut this to
10–20, reorder, and add what's missing. Moment IDs and dates verified against
`/api/v1/moments/{id}/` unless marked otherwise.

Each entry follows the two-worlds structure from `40-in-universe.md`: an in-universe

## Two decisions this list can't make on its own

### 1. The pre-2017 posts have no honest backdate

MT1 is 1983–84. MT2 is around 2008. Backdating a Patreon post to 1984 is probably
not even possible in the editor, and if it were, the post would sit so deep in the
feed that nobody would ever see it. Options:

- **(a)** Date the mythology posts to *now* and let only the MT3 milestones carry
  true historical dates. Breaks "backdate correctly," but only for the prologue.
- **(b)** Put the whole mythology into a single Collection where feed position
  doesn't matter much, dated wherever the editor allows.
- **(c)** Skip the prologue on Patreon entirely; keep MT1/MT2 for the website.

### 2. Some of the best material isn't a Moment

The rotating stage, the unopened box, the lost first track, François's kick — none
of these exist as rows in `moments`. They come from the canon docs and have no
`moment_date`. Each needs a date chosen by hand, or a decision to post it undated at
whatever point in the drip feels right.

## Notes on ordering

The drip order and the feed order are different things. If posts are backdated
correctly, the feed arranges them by history no matter when they go out — so the
drip order controls only what the *notifications* say. Sending them in chronological
order means friends receive the story in sequence even though the archive fills in
behind them.

Suggested: prologue first if it's included at all, then chronological, with S3
("there is no second take") held back as the closer.

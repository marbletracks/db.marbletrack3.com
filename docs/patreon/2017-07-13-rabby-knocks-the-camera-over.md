Rabby is enormous, very floppy, and hard to work with. He was also, six days into
filming, headed straight at the camera.

Should he look inside it? Nah, too difficult to film. But I had to get him past
somehow. Ah — he can knock the camera out of the way!

That took hours of filming to get the camera *back* into the correct spot.

On his way off the set he also crushed Squarehead. Why? No one knows. I thought it
was funny to have a big animal innocently crush the guy, and even funnier that he
could just get up again and be okay.

Rabby's own account of the incident, for the record: *"Not my fault the camera
operator panicked as I hit the camera."*

Lin suggested I cut the whole thing. I was like "no way!" after I spent so long
filming it.

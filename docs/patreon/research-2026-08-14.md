## 2017 — the first year

### Filming may have started 2017-07-04, not 07-07

`content/episode/2017/07/04mt3-have-a-plan-for-the-base.md` is described as the
first day of filmed frames and livestream. By `07/07got-my-dragonframe-bluetooth-keypad.md`
Rob writes *"Yesterday I ended up with about 80 frames of completed video."*

But DB moment 1 puts frames 1–14 of **take 3** at 2017-07-07.

**Question for Rob:** were the early-July frames test takes (takes 1, 2 and 4 are
all labelled tests), with take 3 — the real one — starting on the 7th? The backdate
for the opening post depends on the answer.

Also on 07-04: *"Here is the video of just the frames of characters moving around.
Red/white guy escapes the stage."* — a character predating the named cast.
YouTube: `98mAchukT_4`, `xIqI629wvT0`, `-tMTvzePnu0`.

### The opening scene — narrative source

`content/snippets/0010_setting_the_stage.md` (snippet 1, YouTube `-KjsYc4Mb5g`):

> "Squarehead leads Rabby onto the set as Rabby drags a round circular plate behind him."
> "Squarehead unties the ropes from Rabby."
> "Rabby squishes Squarehead as he heads toward the camera, knocking it off its stand."
> "By the time the camera is reset, Squarehead has recovered..."

New detail: **Rabby was roped to the plate, and Squarehead unties him.** Settles the
lead-vs-drag question completely — both are true, in sequence.

Image for the stage-moving episode (07-11):
`https://b.robnugen.com/art/marble_track_3/frames/2018/00010_002_03_X1_0124.jpg`

## 2025 — the revival

Thirteen episodes, 29 May – 19 June 2025, then nothing. **No July or August 2025
episodes exist**, so the later 2025 moments have no website coverage at all.

### The gap explained — 2025-05-29 (not 06-02)

`content/episode/2025/05/29getting-things-lined-up.md`:

> "After just over three years of chilling, Marble Track 3 construction is back!"
> "In Real Life, I moved, changed jobs, started a book, and more, all while Marble
> Track 3 waited patiently!"

**This is the answer to why the project stopped**, and it was sitting in the archive
the whole time. He moved house, changed jobs, and started a book. The set survived
the move. Studio is in **Yurigaoka**.

Image: `.../construction/2025/2025_jun_02_set_up_in_yurigaoka_studio_1000.jpeg`
YouTube `jySdgLy2qUI`.

### First frames back — 2025-06-02

> "It was quite a thrill once I took the first frame that would be used in the
> video.. though I kept hiding and retaking it six times before I was happy with it."

11 frames shot, 5 kept. YouTube `GG5dw7NPRGI`.

### The worst day — 2025-06-09

Two posts that day. The 3pm one is the failure:

> "Today is the day I forgot to take a photo of the Caret Splitter Feeder Extension
> Reflection Stanchion before ripping it out."
> "I was tempted to just say bah and toss it all."
> "1 frame in 3.75 hours, so 0.265 frames per hour. Woooo!"

And the 5:23pm one: *"Zero frames per hour, a new low! Wooo!"*

**Strong post candidate.** Two streams, one day, 0.265 frames per hour and then
zero, a part ripped out with no before-photo and re-glued a millimetre off, and
Rob cheerful about all of it. YouTube `JSDalruUzq0`, `ZlVd34qtLRo`.

### The renumbering — 2025-06-19, confirmed

> "The so-called 8th outer spiral support was never a supporter of the outer spiral!"
> "the old site had a page showing Mr Greene bringing a piece to be the 10th placed
> outer spiral support, but I could find no evidence of it actually being placed"

Supports 1–7 were correct; the old "8th" was a Triple Splitter Small Feeder Y
Support; the old "10th" was never placed at all. Four good images, all
`2025_06_19_*`. YouTube `Ikr2hViZS3Y`.

  alligator-clip sticks**, because hanging him on thread "proved to be so fiddly."
  Thread bought at Pandora House, Shin-Yurigaoka. YouTube `bNtQb-ukBG4`.
- **2025-06-08**: the naming irony — the 19th and 20th Outer Spiral Supports were

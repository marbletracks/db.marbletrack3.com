I tried to start Marble Track 3 three times before the version you are watching now.

The first was in 2011. I built a tripod for my waterproof camera, waited for the
glue to dry, put down a piece of wood, and shot some frames.

There was apparently a second attempt, but I have no biological memory of it. The
only evidence of that era is this video, labelled "Third attempt," from May 2013. A
minute and nine seconds. That's all I had to show for it — but it was a great start!

Two things killed those attempts.

The first was the table. The stage was fixed to it, which meant I couldn't reach
round to the back of the project without knocking something over. Every session was
either uncomfortable or destructive, and usually both.

The second is worse, and I still think about it. On one of those attempts I had to
give up because the frames were inaccessible — I had encrypted the backup, and then
I lost the password. I was so afraid someone would steal the hard work! I was sure I
was smart enough to keep a password safe.

Nobody stole them. Nobody can look at them either.

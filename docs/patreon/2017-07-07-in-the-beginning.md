The first thing that happens in Marble Track 3 is nothing at all.

Fourteen frames — a bit over a second, at twelve frames per second — of an empty
field. No workers, no track, no marbles. Just the place where a marble track is
going to be.

I shot them on 7 July 2017. **[NEED: what the "field" physically was — bare wooden
stage? paper? cloth? something green? And is anything moving in those fourteen
frames, or is it a held shot?]**

It had taken a while to get there. I tried to start this in 2011 and gave up, and
that track doesn't exist anywhere any more. The thing that made this attempt
possible was a bearing I bought in 2012 — a lazy Susan, the kind that goes under a
Chinese restaurant table — because the first attempt had taught me that I couldn't
reach the back of my own project without picking the whole thing up.

**[NEED: what it felt like to start again after 2011. Were you confident this one
would work, or was it just another go?]**

Meanwhile, in the story: this is a plot of land where a park is going to be built.
The crew hasn't arrived. The residents haven't moved in. Squarehead is about to walk
in backwards, leading Rabby, who is carrying the base — and who will, six days
later, knock the camera over and let everyone know it wasn't his fault.

Fourteen frames of nothing is a strange thing to be proud of. But nine years later,
it's still the only day the track was ever empty.

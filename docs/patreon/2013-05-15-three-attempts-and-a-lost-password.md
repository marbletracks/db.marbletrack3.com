I had three failed attempts to make an animation of the construction of Marble Track 3. Above is the third one, a minute and 9 seconds!

The first was in 2011. I built a tripod for my waterproof camera, waited for the glue to dry, put down a piece of wood, and shot some frames.

There was apparently a second attempt, but I have no biological memory of it. The only evidence of that era is this video, labelled "Third attempt," from May 2013.

In all of these, I came to the same fundamental problem: the stages couldn't rotate as part of the movie. In their world, the stage is fixed; if I wanted to move it between frames, I would have to place it back in just the right spot, and re-place all the characters. I can't just yell "places, everyone!"
